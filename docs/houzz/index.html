Find contractors and design studios on Houzz

Build a local trade list for a supplier or agency. Each of the two city searches starts with the location and filters set, returns up to five matching profiles, and exports to CSV or JSON.

Los Angeles contractors

For building-materials suppliers and marketing agencies looking for a published business email.

  • Search general contractors serving the Los Angeles area. Every match includes an email and its source.
  • In one LA area search before filtering, 38 of 60 profiles had an email published on the business website.
Try the LA contractor search

Chicago design studios

For furniture, lighting and textile reps choosing interior design studios for a trade presentation.

  • Find studios serving Chicago with a website and at least three Houzz reviews. An email is optional.
  • Open the studio website to check its portfolio, then use the Houzz profile to read its reviews.
Try the Chicago search

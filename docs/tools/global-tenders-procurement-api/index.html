WHY THIS DATASET

A procurement pipeline built for matching, alerts, and market intelligence

The hard part is not finding a portal. It is reconciling identifiers, dates, currencies, buyer names, award states, and reuse terms across jurisdictions. This Actor does that normalization before records reach your workflow.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

        
What buyers ask before the first run.

        
Which sources are included?
The unified Actor covers UK Find a Tender, EU TED, Grants.gov, USASpending, World Bank procurement, Canada contracts, and AusTender.
Are award values converted to one currency?
No. The valueAmount field stays in the currency published by the source, which avoids hidden exchange-rate assumptions.
Can one source time out without failing the run?
Yes. Sources run concurrently behind independent timeout and error boundaries. Available results continue through normalization and deduplication.
When should I use the USASpending companion Actor?
Use it when your workflow needs only U.S. federal contracts, grants, or IDVs and you want dedicated US filters at a lower per-record rate.

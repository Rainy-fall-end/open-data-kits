FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

        
What buyers ask before the first run.

        
What is included in a drug dossier?
A dossier can include active-trial totals, phase distribution, top sponsors, recent trials, FDA approval and recall totals, ChEMBL identity and properties, ATC codes, literature totals, and source status.
Can I query a disease instead of a drug?
Yes. The query accepts a drug, molecule, or indication. Broad or ambiguous terms should be reviewed against the included official-source links before decisions are made.
Does the output include investigator or patient contacts?
No. The Actor does not request investigator, responsible-party, or location-contact fields, and extracted text is scrubbed for contact strings.
Is this medical advice?
No. It is a research and intelligence assembly tool. Source matching can be ambiguous, and scientific, regulatory, investment, or clinical decisions require review of the official records.
